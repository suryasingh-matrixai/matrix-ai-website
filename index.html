Research

Six tracks. One thesis: AI that acts, not just answers.

We work across the full stack of applied AI — from decision systems that trade in real markets to the context infrastructure that keeps enterprise agents grounded, accurate, and safe.

AI for Trading

Quantitative and generative models for signal discovery, execution, and risk — built for real capital, real latency, and real drawdowns.

Agentic AI

Multi-step, tool-using agents that plan, act, and self-correct — designed to operate reliably inside real workflows, not just demos.

Generative AI

Foundation-model applications spanning language, structured reasoning, and multimodal generation, tuned for domain-specific accuracy.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Retrieval and grounding pipelines that keep models factual — hybrid search, re-ranking, and evaluation built for production-scale corpora.

Context Engineering

The discipline of feeding models the right information, at the right time, in the right shape — memory, state, and context-window strategy.

Enterprise Agentic Platform

Infrastructure for deploying, orchestrating, and governing fleets of agents across an organization — observability, permissions, and control built in.

About

Matrix AI Research

We're a research organization building at the intersection of applied machine learning, financial markets, and enterprise software. Our team works on problems where AI has to make decisions with consequences — not just generate plausible text.

Matrix AI Research operates as an independent lab: we publish, we prototype, and we deploy. Our scope spans quantitative trading systems, autonomous agents, generative model applications, and the context and retrieval infrastructure that makes enterprise AI trustworthy at scale.
